Ostriches

The ostrich (Struthio camelus) is a species of large flightless bird native to Africa. It is the largest living bird in the world and lays the largest eggs of any living land animal.

Speed & Strength

Ostriches can sprint at over 70 km/h (43 mph), making them the fastest running birds on earth. They can maintain a steady speed of about 50 km/h (31 mph) over long distances.

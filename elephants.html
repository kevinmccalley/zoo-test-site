About African Elephants

The African elephant is a marvel of nature.

Key Facts

• Average weight: 6,000 kg
• Lifespan: 60–70 years
• Diet: Herbivore (grass, fruit, bark)
• Habitat: African savanna and forests
⚠ FEEDING TIME AT 3PM TODAY!

Did You Know?

Elephants are the only animals that cannot jump. They use their trunks, which contain over 40,000 muscles, to breathe, smell, drink, and grab objects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I feed the elephants?
Yes! Elephant feeding sessions are held daily at 3pm. You can purchase elephant food at the main gift shop for $5.
How close can I get to the elephants?
Our elephant enclosure has a viewing platform 3 meters from the fence. During feeding sessions, guests may approach within 1 meter under staff supervision.
Are elephants dangerous?
Our elephants are well-trained and accustomed to human presence, but as wild animals they should always be respected and observed from a safe distance.

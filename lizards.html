Lizards

The Reptile House

Lizards are a widespread group of squamate reptiles with over 6,000 species, ranging across all continents except Antarctica. They range from tiny geckos a few centimetres long to the Komodo dragon at 3 metres.

Species in Our Collection

Species highlighted in red are venomous. Species in green are safe to handle.

  • Gila Monster (venomous)
  • Blue-tongued Skink (safe to handle)
  • Komodo Dragon (venomous)
  • Bearded Dragon (safe to handle)
  • Leopard Gecko (safe to handle)
  • Mexican Beaded Lizard (venomous)
